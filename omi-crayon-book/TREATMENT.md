# يومٌ عاديّ بقي في القلب — Crayon Picture Book

Style test 2 of 3 for the Hikaytomi Reels series «أمي، احكي لي». It uses the same on-screen script and timing as the one-line and urban-sketch versions (`omi-shared/timeline.json`). Only the style changes.

## Brief (as agreed)

- **Source:** Q235. The printed question «ما اللحظة التي لو عادت، لتمنّيتِ أن تعيشيها بنفس الشعور مرة أخرى؟» is adapted on screen to «ما اللحظة التي تتمنّين أن تعيشيها مرّةً أخرى؟».
- **Form:** an emotional reflection. The family is a **fictional vignette** and implies nothing about any real mother's answer. Mum is alive and present at the end: no empty chair.
- **No voice.** The book's printed words are on screen (Modern Standard Arabic), with crayon/brush foley and a small wooden-and-metal score.
- **Format:** 1080×1920 at 24 fps, about 56 s. Bare paper at the bottom of each page holds the printed words (y 1290–1560), clear of Instagram's overlay zones.
- **Ending:** a soft call to action with the real «حكاية أمي» book mockup and hikaytomi.com. No price.
- **Facts:** none claimed.


> **Sound, as delivered:** following the request to use on-screen writing and sound effects, the main cut (`omi-crayon-book.mp4`) has effects and ambience only. The score described below is kept as an alternate cut (`omi-crayon-book-music.mp4`), so you can compare the two.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Two pages and a secret (chosen).** Page one is drawn live: the lunch. A child is mid-game as the call to eat comes, and one child is late. A page turn takes us to years later: the same table, the children now tall. Their height marks on the door frame get coloured in, and a laugh scribbles pink across the faces. A second page turn takes us to the present: a grown daughter beside Mum on the sofa, tea in hand. From the first frame of that page, the question has been written in **white wax**, invisible. When the question beat arrives, a rose watercolour wash sweeps the page and the wax resists it: «ما اللحظة…» appears out of the colour. Then we pull out past the page edge: the page is one page of a book on a table, next to the real «حكاية أمي». *Why:* "the question was always there; you just have to ask" is exactly what wax resist does, and the book pull-out is the medium's own way into the product.
2. **A colouring-book page.** All outlines are present from frame one and each beat colours one area. Rejected: there's no time passing, so the "you grew up" turn has nowhere to live.
3. **A crumpled page flattened.** The crumple is the years. Rejected: crumpling reads as neglect or loss, which is the wrong emotion.

## Logline and arc

A picture-book page draws an ordinary lunch, turns into the years, and ends on a question that was hidden in the page all along.

- **Setup (0–10.3):** blank paper. Crayon draws a window, a sun, and a little girl mid-throw with a ball. The hook words are printed on the page; then «قد تختار يومًا عاديًّا جدًّا» while the rug and toys are coloured.
- **Development (10.3–20.6):** Mum in the doorway with a wooden spoon, a coloured speech-squiggle calling. The table is coloured; two children run to it. The third keeps stacking blocks, with substitution poses (stack, stack, look up, run).
- **Turn (20.6–31.4):** page turn. The same table, years later, coloured in new crayons, the children now tall. The door frame's height marks are coloured on, rising. The laugh: pink scribbles on every cheek, "ha" squiggles in the air.
- **Breath (31.4–33):** the boil calms to half. Quiet.
- **Ending (33–49.5):** page turn to the present: daughter and Mum on the sofa, two cups. Mum is talking, with a speech bubble holding a tiny crayon picture of the old lunch. At 38.1 the rose wash sweeps right to left, and the white-wax question appears out of it. Then the follow-up is printed.
- **Call to action (49.8–55.8):** pull out past the page edge. The page lies in an open book on a warm table, and the real book mockup sits beside it. Brand text is hand-lettered on.

## Benchmark

- **Learn from** Raymond Briggs' *The Snowman*: still backgrounds with living figures, wordless tenderness, one tune carried through. Also contemporary Arabic children's picture books for the warmth of domestic scenes.
- **Don't take:** any character, the song, or imagery.

## Type

The printed words use **Marhey**, a rounded hand-drawn Arabic face that fills the role of Patrick Hand. It's set at 64 px in the outline colour (plum `#5a2e3a`), right-aligned per line and centred as a block, with each glyph cluster tilted and offset slightly and boiling on twos. Text is written right to left on a ragged knock-out patch of paper. The wax-resist question is pre-drawn in white wax with the same face at 74 px. The call to action's brand text uses **El Messiri** (store typeface).
