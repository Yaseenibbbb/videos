# Hikaytomi: Instagram Reels creative plan
_Date: 27 October 2026. Creative hypotheses for testing, not predictions of reach._

## Start here

Build a small Arabic storytelling channel about the lives our mothers lived, the details families inherit, and the questions that open a conversation.

**Flagship series: «قبل ما تصير أمي» — Before she became Mum.**

The audience promise: every episode reveals one unexpected detail about a mother's life and leaves you with one worthwhile question to ask your own mother. People should enjoy the film even if they never buy the journal. The product gives people a way to keep the conversations they begin.

Recommended first production: the three pilots below, one per requested visual style. These are different creative treatments, not a controlled test of which style performs best. Do not render a whole season before reviewing the pilots.

## Audience and voice

Start with Arabic-speaking adults who enjoy family stories, recognise ordinary Arab home life, or want to know their mother better. Sons and daughters are both included. Mothers can also watch and recognise their own earlier selves.

- Write natural, accessible spoken Arabic. Pilot drafts use light colloquial Arabic; review the voice read before locking a dialect.
- Keep the adult child unnamed and off-screen for the initial pilots. First-person phrases such as «أمي» and «سألتها» do not make the viewer a son or a daughter.
- Give mothers humour, preferences, ambitions, friends and agency. Do not make every mother a self-sacrificing saint.
- Start with accessible memories. Deeper prompts belong later, when the channel has established warmth.
- Make sharing a response to the story, not a condition for seeing its ending.
- Fiction is described in the caption as «حكاية متخيّلة، مستوحاة من سؤال في حكاية أمي». Do not present invented dialogue as a customer testimonial or a true account of the founder's mother.
- Let people answer privately or skip a question. A family-memory account should not pressure viewers to disclose painful experiences.

## Five repeatable series

| Series | Story engine | Why someone might return | Best starting style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **قبل ما تصير أمي** | An ordinary detail reveals a surprising chapter of her earlier life. | Discover another side of the same mother; recognise their own family. | One-line |
| **أمي كانت مشاغبة** | A present-day family rule meets the funny childhood story of the person who enforces it. | Warm, recognisable comedy with a real punchline. | Crayon-book |
| **أماكن تحفظنا** | A smell, doorway, street or recipe reconstructs a place someone carries inside them. | Visit another remembered place; share their own. | Urban-sketch |
| **سؤال فتح حكاية** | One question leads to one concrete scene, ending with a version the viewer can ask. | Leave with a usable conversation starter every time. | One-line or urban-sketch |
| **أشياء ورثناها بدون ما ننتبه** | Follow a gesture, phrase, recipe or small habit through generations. | Notice something familiar in a new way. | One-line or crayon-book |

Launch with the flagship and the playful childhood strand. Keep the other formats available as variation rather than introducing five separate shows at once.

## A storyline that can last

A fictional mother, **ليلى**, appears across episodes. Her adult child keeps discovering that familiar objects contain unfamiliar stories.

The first six episodes loosely uncover: her nickname, childhood mischief, closest friend, first meaningful choice, early motherhood, and a wish she still has. There is no compulsory viewing order. Every film resolves its own question.

Continuity comes from recurring details: the same hairstyle silhouette at each age, a plum cardigan in the colour styles, a chipped teacup, a particular gate. One-line expresses the same identity through silhouette and line, not coloured clothing fills.

Do not force this character onto every film. Place stories and inherited habits can stand alone. Later, a separate mini-season could follow another fictional family, with a distinct setting.

## Shape of a Reel

Use roughly 25–40 seconds initially; length follows the story and the recorded voice.

1. **0–2 seconds: curiosity in motion.** A specific question or discrepancy, plus a meaningful visual already happening. No logo introduction.
2. **2–10 seconds: a concrete setup.** An object, action or person. Avoid a paragraph of abstract reflection.
3. **10–25 seconds: discovery.** Something happens that changes what the viewer thought.
4. **25–32 seconds: emotional or comic payoff.** Let the drawing and a brief pause carry it.
5. **Final 3–5 seconds: one invitation.** A question to ask Mum, or an audience memory prompt. Most films end here without the product.

The question should emerge from the story. Do not read a journal prompt over unrelated decorative animation. The animation must perform the action or reveal information.

## Pilot 01 — مين كان يناديها «لولو»؟

**Series:** قبل ما تصير أمي  
**Style:** one-line  
**Target:** 30–34 seconds, adjusted after the voice read  
**Source:** printed pp.7 and 19  
**Purpose:** establish the channel's central promise without showing the product.

### Arabic voice draft

كلنا نناديها ماما.

بس مرة، رنّ تلفونها…
وسمعت وحدة تقول: «لولو!»

ضحكت أمي ضحكة ما أسمعها كثير.
وسألتها: «مين لولو؟»

قالت: «أنا… قبل ما تصيرون تنادوني ماما.»

وبدأت تحكي عن صاحبتها،
وعن طريق المدرسة،
وعن أشياء ما كنت أعرفها.

إيش كان اسم دلع أمك؟
ومين كان يناديها فيه؟

### Visual treatment

| Time | Picture / story action | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Already mid-drawing: one continuous line forms a telephone receiver. Hook appears in a blank margin. | Soft ring and pen scratch; voice begins immediately. |
| 3–8s | The receiver cord extends into Mum's profile. A slight lifted chin and shoulder suggest recognition and laughter. | Friend's «لولو» is warm, not a startling shout. |
| 8–16s | Continue along a natural connector into a school gate and two younger figures. No cut, erasure or second independently animated line. | One solo instrument begins a playful phrase. |
| 16–25s | Camera follows the same path along a short school route; the two figures lean together, suggesting a shared joke. | Leave room after the central line; drawing remains audible. |
| 25–34s | A composed view connects the older and younger identities through the uninterrupted path. Question holds in clear paper. Do not force an elaborate face-shaped final reveal. | Phrase resolves softly; final question stays readable. |

**Caption draft:**  
حكاية متخيّلة، مستوحاة من سؤال في حكاية أمي. أحيانًا اسم صغير يفتح حكاية كاملة. إيش كان اسم دلع أمك؟

**Ending:** question only. No book, URL, price or shopping instruction.

## Pilot 02 — أمي كانت مشاغبة

**Series:** أمي كانت مشاغبة  
**Style:** crayon-book  
**Target:** 28–34 seconds  
**Source:** printed pp.9–10  
**Purpose:** demonstrate that this channel can make people laugh, and give the mother a personality beyond caregiving.

### Arabic voice draft

أمي تقول: «لا تلعبون بالكرة في البيت.»

بس يوم سألتها عن طفولتها…
ضحكت.

طلعت هي تسوي مرمى من أحذية أختها،
والكرة تدخل تحت الطاولة كل شوي.

قلت: «وجدتي كانت تعرف؟»

قالت: «أكيد لا!»

وبعدها طالعت الأحذية اللي تحت طاولتنا…
وضحكت مرة ثانية.

إيش المشاغبة اللي أمك للحين تضحك عليها؟

### Visual treatment

| Time | Picture / story action | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | A crayon ball rolls toward two pairs of shoes under a present-day table. A foot stops it; Mum's line lands. | Ball taps, then a woodblock stop. |
| 4–10s | Page turn into childhood. Young Layla draws the two shoe-goalposts into place through the colouring action. | Crayon swishes and a small playful woodwind phrase. |
| 10–19s | Two clear substitution poses: kick, then an exaggerated crouch to retrieve the ball beneath the table. | One comic pause before «أكيد لا!». |
| 19–25s | Page turn back to the present. The same arrangement of shoes is visible. Mum notices; expression changes to amusement. | Theme returns, warmer. |
| 25–34s | Hold on her smile and the ball; final question in the reserved paper area. | Music settles rather than adding a sad ending. |

**Art direction:** toothy warm paper, plum or indigo outlines, restrained crayon palette, lively hand-drawn pose changes. No glossy vector people with a texture filter. No need for the optional watercolour-resist effect in this story.

**Caption draft:**  
حكاية متخيّلة، مستوحاة من أسئلة الطفولة في حكاية أمي. لكل بيت حكاية مع «لا تلعبون بالكرة»… حتى بيت أمهاتنا زمان.

**Ending:** question only.

## Pilot 03 — ريحة ترجع البيت

**Series:** أماكن تحفظنا  
**Style:** urban-sketch  
**Target:** 30–36 seconds  
**Source:** printed p.41, supported by p.7  
**Purpose:** test a sensory, place-led film with less dialogue and more atmospheric storytelling.

### Arabic voice draft

سألت أمي: «إيش أكثر ريحة تذكّرك ببيتكم؟»

ما جاوبت على طول.

فتحت علبة الهيل،
وقرّبتها مني.

قالت: «أمي كانت تحطه في الشاي…
وأعرف إنها صحيت قبل ما أسمع صوتها.»

فجأة، صار للبيت اللي ما شفته
صوت… وريحة… وصباح.

لو سألت أمك نفس السؤال،
وين بتاخذك الحكاية؟

### Visual treatment

| Time | Picture / story action | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Close on a sketched tin opening beside a cup. One small wash of colour appears around the cardamom. | Tin lid; question spoken naturally. |
| 5–12s | Pen lines sketch the remembered kitchen and doorway. Colour arrives from the cup and spreads into the space. | Kettle and distant birds begin before the place is fully revealed. |
| 12–23s | Pan into the courtyard: young Layla and her mother share the morning. Local colour follows the memory; margins stay bare paper. | A spoon against a cup; minimal music. |
| 23–29s | Page turn back to today's table, with the same cup-giving gesture repeated by adult Layla. | The cup sound bridges time. |
| 29–36s | Hold on two cups and the final question. | Allow a breath before the ending. |

**Art direction:** sepia fineliner plus loose transparent wash, imperfect registration, frayed painted edges. Choose a coherent fictional home rather than mixing unrelated Arab architectural symbols. No dissolves into photorealistic footage.

**Caption draft:**  
حكاية متخيّلة، مستوحاة من سؤال في حكاية أمي عن رائحة بيت الطفولة. لو للبيت ريحة واحدة، إيش بتكون؟

**Ending:** question only. A later companion post can demonstrate recording a real answer in the journal.

## How the book enters naturally

Sell the practice of noticing and keeping a story. The journal is a useful object in that practice.

For the first 12 posts, try this editorial mix:

- **9 complete stories:** no product in the film. The account name and profile establish the brand.
- **2 practical companion films:** show how to turn a conversation into a kept memory; the journal can appear as a genuine example.
- **1 clear product film:** what the journal contains, how someone uses it, and the invitation to order.

These proportions are a starting choice, not a proven formula. Do not bolt a shopping end card onto every touching moment.

Example companion film: the story has already resolved; a separate film asks «سمعت الحكاية… كيف تحفظها؟». Show a short answer being written beside its corresponding prompt. Use a readable, verified book asset when available, rather than inventing the cover.

Possible soft caption line, reserved for companion posts:  
«إذا فتحت الحكاية، ممكن تحفظونها كتابة. حكاية أمي يجمع أسئلة تساعد على البداية.»

Keep one pinned introduction to the journal and a profile destination for people who look for it. Let ordinary story posts use their ending for the story. Do not make website, delivery, price or stock claims until checked against the current store.

## First month: a small, sustainable experiment

Suggested cadence: three posts a week, only if production quality makes that manageable.

| Week | Post 1 | Post 2 | Post 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pilot 01: the nickname | Pilot 02: childhood mischief | Pilot 03: cardamom and the remembered home |
| 2 | School friend, ID 04 | Eid shoes, ID 05 | First meaningful choice, adapted from ID 16 |
| 3 | Early motherhood, ID 08 | Family phrase, ID 10 | Companion: keeping one answer in writing |
| 4 | A current dream, ID 12 | Companion: how one prompt becomes a family conversation | Clear journal introduction |

This keeps nine story films, two companion films and one product film. Keep cultural or seasonal episodes tied to memory unless publishing them at the relevant season.

### What to learn

Look at each post after a similar amount of time, for example seven days:

- Average watch time relative to duration; completion and early drop-off where those insights are available.
- Shares, saves and follows relative to reach; use available post-attributed follows when shown.
- Specific responses: are viewers remembering their own family, asking their mother, or only commenting on the animation?
- Profile visits and, where attributable, website visits and orders.

Calculate comparable rates per 1,000 reached rather than comparing only raw counts. Treat small samples as directional. Do not choose a permanent style because one unrelated story had more views.

If a film gets attention for its drawing but produces little recognition or conversation, strengthen the human situation. If a question gets saved but the opening loses people, change the first action or line. If product posts get visits but few orders, inspect the store journey separately before rewriting every story.

For a later style comparison, use matched topics and similar lengths across several posts, or compare the same storyboard in private previews. The first three pilots test creative fit and feasibility together.

### Shared production notes

- Preserve one visual style within each film. Across films, shared voice, warm paper and the series premise provide continuity.
- Design for vertical composition from the start; do not crop a completed 16:9 film.
- Use a working central area for important objects and subtitles; check the current Instagram preview before publication because overlay placement can vary.
- Keep mother and key objects large enough to read on a phone. Do not spend the opening watching an empty page.
- One-line: use uninterrupted drawing and natural connecting paths; text can be a separate shaped caption layer.
- Crayon: maintain tactile wax and pose changes. Avoid a smooth corporate animation that only has grain added.
- Urban: preserve both ink and wash, and use colour arrival to communicate memory.
- Choose sound for the style and scene. Do not use the same melancholy piano bed on every film.
- Do not copy copyrighted music or upstream demo audio. Record or generate suitable new audio with appropriate usage rights.
- Do not upload or publish to Instagram as part of a render request.
