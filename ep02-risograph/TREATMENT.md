# انتصار صغير — Risograph Print (Episode 02, Q203)

## Brief (from `content/episodes/02-quiet-pride.md`)

- **Question (Q203), printed only at the end:** «ما الإنجاز الذي تفخرين به أكثر في حياتكِ، حتى لو لم يصفّق له أحد؟»
- **Story:** a **fictional vignette**. A mother learns to ride a bicycle with her grown son steadying the saddle, then rides a short way alone.
- **Sound:** effects and ambience only. No voice, no music, no singing, no human vocal sounds. No print-machine foley either: it would be a sound with no cause in the story.
- **Text:** the question only, at the end, on a paper-white knockout.
- **Product:** not shown.
- **Format:** vertical 1080×1920, about 38 s. Characters act on twos, the camera moves on ones.

Shared beat sheet: `omi-shared/ep02-timeline.json`, the same as the watercolor version.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Plates as narrative (chosen).** While she wobbles and tries, the world is printed in **one ink** (Federal Blue): sky tint, ground solid, figures as blue silhouettes. When his hand comes, a second plate arrives with a roller sweep: **Sunflower**, as the low sun and the warm light on the ground. When she rides on alone, the third plate arrives: **Red**, her cardigan and the bike frame, and with it the first full overprint. Registration drifts while she hesitates and snaps back on the push-off. *Why:* each ink is a step of courage, and the full overprint is saved for the moment that earns it.
2. **Two inks in dialogue.** Her in red, him in blue, overlapping into violet when he holds the saddle. Rejected: the overlap would make the help the peak, but the peak is her riding *alone*.
3. **A zine of attempts.** Each try is a page with a different ink pairing. Rejected: page cuts break the continuous ride the brief wants to see.

## Logline and arc

A small figure in a big blue square: on each brave step the print gains an ink, until she rides away in full colour.

- **Wobble (0–4):** ground-level low angle, a big front-wheel silhouette, her shoe planted. One ink only, registration slightly loose.
- **Tries (4–10):** static wide: a horizon, a huge sun disc printed as a knockout (blank paper), a tiny figure. She rolls a little and stops. At 8.6–9.6 the plates drift off register in silence.
- **Help (10–17):** a medium two-shot. He walks in; his hand on the saddle snaps the registration back. The **Sunflower** plate rolls in: the disc fills, light on the ground.
- **Alone (17–24):** wide lateral tracking. The bike rolls and his hand lets go. At the release (≈20 s) the **Red** plate arrives with a kick: her cardigan, the frame, a few roofs. Full overprint.
- **Pride (24–30):** a locked composition: horizon, disc, figure. She brakes, looks back to him, small and far away, and smiles. The registration holds tight.
- **Question (30–38):** the camera holds wide. A paper-white knockout strip opens across the sky and the question is printed on the blue plate.

## Benchmark

- **Learn from** Tom Haugomat's riso illustrations (a small figure in a large flat world, overprinted colour fields) and Tati's *Jour de fête* (everyday action staged small in a wide frame, everyday sounds as rhythm).
- **Don't take:** any composition, character or palette.

## Type

The question uses **Readex Pro** (an OFL Arabic geometric sans, in the role of the style's Jost) at weight 600 in Federal Blue. It is printed on one plate, so it grains and misregisters with the print, on a paper-white knockout strip.

## Inks

Federal Blue `#3D5588` (the silhouette plate: everything readable in one ink), Sunflower `#FFB511`, Red `#FF665E`. Paper `#F4EEE2`. Skin is a light Sunflower tint once that plate exists, and paper until then.
