## Three candidate structures

1. **A handscroll that paints itself ahead of her (chosen).** The square is one long horizontal painted strip. While she wobbles, only the ground under her wheels is painted; the rest is paper. Each try paints a little more road. Once his hand leaves the saddle, the brush runs ahead of her: the path, a tree, the far wall paint themselves as she rides into them, so the distance she covers is literally new painting. At the stop, the camera pulls back over everything that got painted. Then mist washes it back to paper, leaving the bicycle and the question. *Why:* "painting-in as reveal" makes the achievement visible as painted distance. The scroll is the medium's long lateral dolly.
2. **Specimen + note.** The bicycle as a botanical plate, with parts labelled. Rejected: the brief forbids text before the question.
3. **A journal page per try.** Three locked pages, one per attempt. Rejected: page turns break the continuous balance the brief asks to see.

## Logline and arc

A mother wobbles, tries, borrows a steadying hand, then rides on alone while the world paints itself in front of her wheel.

- **Wobble (0–4):** a slow push on the front wheel and her shoe touching down. Only that patch of ground is painted.
- **Tries (4–10):** wider. The quiet square is barely sketched: a bench, a tree trunk, a far wall in pale washes. She looks at the pedal, pushes off, rolls about a bike length and stops. At 8.6–9.6 the brush lifts and everything goes quiet.
- **Help (10–17):** her son walks in from the right, painted stroke by stroke, and puts a hand on the saddle. They exchange a look. Her feet find both pedals.
- **Alone (17–24):** the lateral dolly starts. The road paints ahead of the front wheel, with one vermilion accent: her scarf. His hand opens; he stops and stays behind, getting smaller.
- **Pride (24–30):** she brakes, puts a foot down and turns. The camera pulls back along the painted distance to him, a small figure far behind. She smiles.
- **Question (30–38):** mist washes the square back to paper, except her and the bicycle, small at the bottom. The question is written above them in a serif hand.
