# يومٌ عاديّ بقي في القلب — Urban Sketch · Pen & Wash

Style test 3 of 3 for the Hikaytomi Reels series «أمي، احكي لي». It uses the same on-screen script and timing as the one-line and crayon-book versions (`omi-shared/timeline.json`). Only the style changes.

## Brief (as agreed)

- **Source:** Q235, adapted on screen to «ما اللحظة التي تتمنّين أن تعيشيها مرّةً أخرى؟» (printed version: «ما اللحظة التي لو عادت، لتمنّيتِ أن تعيشيها بنفس الشعور مرة أخرى؟»).
- **Form:** an emotional reflection. The family is a **fictional vignette**; the mother is alive and present at the end.
- **No voice.** The text is handwritten on the sketchbook page (Modern Standard Arabic), with pen, brush and domestic foley and a quiet jazz-waltz trio.
- **Format:** 1080×1920 at 24 fps, about 56 s. The writing goes in the page's blank lower margin, y 1250–1560.
- **Ending:** a soft call to action with the real «حكاية أمي» book mockup and hikaytomi.com. No price.
- **Facts:** none claimed.

## Three candidate structures

1. **One corner, three times (chosen).** A sketchbook spread draws the same dining corner of a family home three times: the lunch long ago, years later, and today. The ink stays sepia throughout. Colour is withheld: the lunch page starts ink-only and **colour arrives from the window** like noon light, at the moment the children are called to eat. The years page is ink-only, with *one coloured thing*, the same old rose tablecloth folded on a chair. When the laugh is remembered, colour blooms back around the table. The present page (Mum and her grown daughter at the same table with tea) is ink until Mum starts talking. Then the wash spreads from her cup outward: her story colours the room. *Why:* "colour arrives with possibility" (from the brief), and the same corner drawn across time is reportage drawing's natural way to show years passing.
2. **A travel diary through the house.** One room per page. Rejected: it's a tour, not a memory, and it has no turn.
3. **A street, from the school gate home.** Rejected: it's outdoors, while the brief's moment is the family table.

## Logline and arc

A sketchbook draws the same corner of a home three times, decades apart, and the colour follows the memory.

- **Setup (0–10.3):** a blank spread with a handwritten corner note «ظهيرة يوم عاديّ». The fineliner draws a window, the table and the rug in real stroke order while the hook text is written in the margin.
- **Development (10.3–20.6):** two children are sketched on the rug and Mum is in the kitchen doorway. Colour arrives from the window (warm noon light) as she calls. The latecomer stays on the rug, still frozen ink while the others are coloured: colour reaches her last, as she gets up.
- **Turn (20.6–31.4):** page turn. The same corner, years later: new lamp, plants grown, height marks on the door frame. All ink, except the folded rose tablecloth. «ضحكة حول المائدة»: colour blooms around the table and the remembered family is sketched over it, laughing.
- **Breath (31.4–33):** a bar of true silence; the pen lifts.
- **Ending (33–49.5):** page turn. Today: Mum and her grown daughter at the table, two glasses of tea. When Mum starts talking, colour spreads from her glass across the page. The question and follow-up are written in the margin.
- **Call to action (49.8–55.8):** pull back to the whole page; the sketchbook closes. On the warm desk beside it lies the real «حكاية أمي». Brand text is handwritten in.

## Benchmark

- **Learn from** urban-sketching sketchbook pages (line first, colour off-register, a date in the corner, frayed page edges) and *Paperman* (one coloured object in a monochrome world).
- **Don't take:** any character, composition or layout from either.

## Type

Everything is handwritten on the page in **Playpen Sans Arabic**, a casual pen hand that fills the role of Caveat, in the ink's sepia `#3b2a1e`. The main lines are written on right to left in the page's lower margin, with pen sound. Corner notes (place, time) are smaller. The call to action's brand text uses **El Messiri** (store typeface) on the desk.

## Palette
